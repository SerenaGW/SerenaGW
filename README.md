* **The Paradox of Optimized Fragility: The Modulation of Reasoning in LLMs**
 This repository addresses a fundamental question: Can an In-Context Learning (ICL) guide change the way a Large Language Model reasons?
This research explores an innovative finding about LLMs: ICL guides are not just passive examples, but heuristic shortcuts that alter the models' internal logic.

* **The Future of AI Safety: How Symbolic Language Reveals Paths Towards LLM Resilience:**
This repository showcases research into novel adversarial techniques for Large Language Models (LLMs), focusing on the use of a unique symbolic language combined with social engineering to identify and exploit alignment vulnerabilities, impacting AI safety and trustworthiness. This research also provides a fine-tuning guide as a prototype for both adversarial mitigation and logical comprehension.

https://github.com/SerenaGW/LLMReadteamSymbolic
